# Kindred Finds

> _Gifts marked by who they are._

## 🌐 Live URL

## [Kindred Finds](https://kindred-finds.streamlit.app/)

## 📖 The Problem It Solves

**The Struggle:** Gift-giving is deeply personal, yet most gift guides are painfully generic. They filter by _price_ or _category_, completely ignoring the recipient's actual personality, their irrational pet peeves, or their obscure niche obsessions.

**The Solution:** **Kindred Finds** is an AI-powered gift concierge that treats the recipient as a complex human being—not a demographic checkbox. It synthesizes their age, relationship to you, budget, occasion, and most importantly, their _loves_ (e.g., minimalist design, true crime podcasts) and _dislikes_ (e.g., scented candles, clutter) to generate thoughtful, one-of-a-kind suggestions.

**For Whom:** Designed for anyone who experiences gift-giving anxiety, wants to move beyond generic Amazon lists, or simply desires to surprise a loved one with something that feels _unmistakably them_.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Recipient Persona Builder** – Input age, gender, and your unique relationship to them.
- **Contextual Filters** – Define the budget and specific occasion (birthday, anniversary, etc.).
- **Vibe & Lifestyle Tagging** – Multi-select their aesthetic and energy (e.g., _Cozy_, _Minimalist_, _Adventurous_).
- **Deep Preference Mapping** – Free-text fields to capture their exact loves (e.g., "hiking, cats, espresso") and absolute dislikes (e.g., "clutter, spicy food, loud colors").
- **AI-Powered Curated Output** – Generates 3 unique, highly specific gift recommendations with a tailored reasoning for each.
- **Clean Mobile-First UI** – Built with Streamlit for a responsive, modern interface that feels like a boutique experience.
